I added a Speaker Theme Explorer that analyzes recurring words across each conference speaker’s talks. It uses TF-IDF (term frequency - inverse document frequency) to highlight words that are frequent for one speaker but less common among other speakers. It ranks if a term is used alot and not used in other speakers talks. It also ranks if it was over multiple talks the one speaker gave instead of just one. The feature displays each theme’s mention count, how many talks contain it, and links to example talks. For example, Dieter F. Uchtdorf’s results highlight aviation-related words such as “flying,” “flight,” and “aircraft.”
